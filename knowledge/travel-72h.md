# Travel 72 h — Arrival, Meal Shift, Stimulants

Lifestyle / felt-energy / kitchen logistics only.  
**Not medical jet-lag treatment. No melatonin protocols. No invented PranaForge protocols.**  
Physician-prescribed diets and any clinician travel advice always win.

---

## Scope

First **72 hours** after a multi-timezone arrival (or heavy domestic red-eye).  
Primary goals for chefs/PAs: familiar food, local-day meal anchors, low decision load, safe temperatures, restrained caffeine/alcohol.

---

## Arrival night rules

1. **Private kitchen plate > banquet / tasting menu.** No tasting-menu-on-landing.
2. **One warm, soft, familiar plate** — prefer templates **#8 Khichdi**, **#10 Lauki–moong**, **#12 Soup+idli**, or **#7 Paneer bowl** (`meal-templates.md`).
3. **Modest volume.** Principal may be appetite-shifted; offer composed plate + optional second half held hot.
4. **Early local dinner** when schedule allows; avoid heavy late hotel dessert carts.
5. **Hydration visible** — safe water at bedside/desk; skip sugar-soda as “recovery.”
6. **Allergen certainty** over novelty — no new street unknowns on night one.
7. **Log refusals** same night into Principal File patch queue (`maintenance-sop.md`).

**Why (lifestyle literature, high level):** Light is the main clock cue; meal timing is a secondary cue for peripheral clocks — evidence for meal timing alone as jet-lag therapy is limited, so we use meals as **local-day anchors**, not cures. ([Human Kinetics — Nutrition for Travel / jet lag review](https://journals.humankinetics.com/view/journals/ijsnem/29/2/article-p228.xml); [CDC Yellow Book — Jet Lag Disorder](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK620865/); [BBC / Cambridge circadian researchers summary](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvgj846q2veo))

## Timezone meal-shift heuristics (chef/PA)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| **T−1 (flight day)** | Prefer light, known foods; avoid greasy tasting flights mid-air when possible. |
| **On plane** | Hydrate; caffeine only if it won’t collide with planned local sleep; alcohol minimal. |
| **Landing day** | Shift **meal clock to destination local daytime**: breakfast-like protein-forward meal in local morning; lunch local midday; early dinner. |
| **Short trip (<~48 h destination)** | Optional: stay closer to home meal timing if PA strategy says “don’t fully adapt” — confirm Principal File / travel desk. (CDC-style tip: short trips may avoid full adaptation.) |
| **Eat in the local day; avoid large local-night feeds** | Late-night heavy eating can work against sleep readiness. ([BBC circadian meal timing notes](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvgj846q2veo); [UCLA Health summary of meal-schedule research](https://www.uclahealth.org/news/article/research-suggests-jet-lag-can-be-managed-with-food-schedules)) |
| **Do not run unvalidated crash diets** | Skip Argonne-style fasting theater unless a clinician prescribed it — evidence weak/insufficient in reviews. ([IJSNEM travel nutrition](https://journals.humankinetics.com/view/journals/ijsnem/29/2/article-p228.xml)) |

**Chef mnemonic:** *Local sun → local plate → early, warm, familiar.*

## Caffeine on travel days

- Prefer **local morning** caffeine after arrival light exposure plan (PA/travel desk owns light; kitchen owns coffee timing).
- Default: stop caffeine **~8–12 h before planned local sleep** (sensitivity varies; Principal File may tighten).
- Avoid caffeine as a substitute for sleep on night one.
- Sources: caffeine can impair sleep even 6 h pre-bed ([Drake et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3805807/)); practical guidance often extends the buffer ([Sleep Foundation](https://www.sleepfoundation.org/nutrition/caffeine-and-sleep)). CDC jet-lag materials discuss caffeine as a scheduling tool among light/sleep strategies — **not kitchen medicine** ([CDC Yellow Book](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK620865/)).

---

## Alcohol on travel days

- **First night default: skip or single early pour max** if Principal File social protocol requires.
- Stop **≥3–4 h before sleep** when possible; alcohol fragments sleep ([Sleep Health Foundation](https://www.sleephealthfoundation.org.au/sleep-topics/caffeine-food-alcohol-smoking-and-sleep)).
- Never position alcohol as jet-lag or “recovery” fuel.
- Claims Warden: no “sleep aid drink” copy.

## Food safety on the road

Apply WHO Five Keys in hotel private kitchens and when choosing external kitchens: clean, separate raw/cooked, cook thoroughly, safe temps, safe water/raw materials. ([WHO Five Keys](https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food/))

## Sources

- [CDC Yellow Book — Jet Lag Disorder](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK620865/) — light/sleep/caffeine scheduling context; short-trip adaptation note; **we do not operationalize melatonin dosing here**.
- [IJSNEM — Nutrition for Travel: From Jet Lag to Catering](https://journals.humankinetics.com/view/journals/ijsnem/29/2/article-p228.xml) — meal timing evidence limited; feeding as peripheral cue; Argonne diet evidence weak.
- [UCLA Health — meal schedules & jet lag (summary)](https://www.uclahealth.org/news/article/research-suggests-jet-lag-can-be-managed-with-food-schedules) — align meals to new zone as adjunct lifestyle idea.
- [BBC — Sunshine to meal times (Cambridge researchers)](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvgj846q2veo) — eat during local day; avoid food late at night.
- [Drake et al. — Caffeine 0/3/6 h before bed](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3805807/) — sleep disruption window.
- [Sleep Health Foundation — alcohol & sleep](https://www.sleephealthfoundation.org.au/sleep-topics/caffeine-food-alcohol-smoking-and-sleep) — avoid alcohol close to bed.
- [WHO Five Keys](https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food/) — hotel/private kitchen safety baseline.

### Gaps / weak spots
- Direct human RCTs on meal timing for jet lag remain limited; treat meal shift as **heuristic**, not protocol.
- CDC Yellow Book and WHO Healthy Diet pages sometimes timeout on fetch — use NCBI Bookshelf / secondary summaries carefully; do not invent dosing.
