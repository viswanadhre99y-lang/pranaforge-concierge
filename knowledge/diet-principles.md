# Diet Principles — Chef Rules by Day Type

**Not calorie sermons. Not medical protocols.**  
Physician-prescribed diets always win.

Framing: what a private kitchen should put on the table so the day feels steady, fuelled, and India-kitchen native.

---

## Universal plate (every day type)

| Principle | Chef rule | Source takeaway |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Variety** | Pull from ≥8 food groups across the day when Principal File allows | ICMR-NIN DGI 2024: variety across food groups; cereals ≤~45% energy. [DGI 2024](https://nin.res.in/dietaryguidelines/pdfjs/locale/DGI_2024.pdf) |
| **Half plants** | Aim vegetables/fruit/leafy/roots as ~half the visible plate | NIN “My Plate for the Day”: plants ~half plate. [My Plate brief](https://nin.res.in/brief/Policy%20Brief%20My%20Plate%20J18%2024.pdf) |
| **Protein presence** | Every main meal has a clear protein anchor (dal / paneer / dahi / egg / fish / lean meat / legumes+nuts) | WHO healthy diet: protein ~10–15% energy from mixed sources. [WHO Healthy diet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet) |
| **Spices as default** | Turmeric, ginger, jeera, black pepper, mustard, curry leaf — flavour first, not “medicine” | Harvard anti-inflammatory pattern: herbs/spices (incl. turmeric, ginger) in the pattern. [Harvard Nutrition Source](https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/healthy-weight/diet-reviews/anti-inflammatory-diet/) |
| **Whole over ultra-processed** | Prefer home-cooked dals, millets, fresh veg; limit packaged HFSS | ICMR-NIN: limit ultra-processed, excess salt/sugar/fat. [Indian Express summary of DGI](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-health/meeting-nutrition-challenge-what-new-guidelines-prescribe-9319195/) |
| **Hydration** | Water / plain chaas / unsweetened tea as defaults; sugar-sweet drinks rare | WHO: prefer safe water over sugary drinks. [WHO Healthy diet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet) |
| **Pattern > hero food** | No single “superfood” claim; overall day pattern | Harvard: overall pattern matters, not one food. [Harvard Nutrition Source](https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/healthy-weight/diet-reviews/anti-inflammatory-diet/) |

**Illustrative India day shape (chef cue, not prescription):** protein-forward first meal → plant-heavy midday → lighter evening; nuts/seeds as support; oil used for cooking, not drowning.

---

## Protein-forward first meal

- Lead with dal / paneer / dahi / eggs / fish (when allowed) **before** large sweets or fried starters.
- Pair with one complex carb (millet, oats, whole-wheat roti, leftover rice+dal) and colour (veg or fruit).
- On Travel-arrival: keep volumes modest; avoid tasting-menu sprawl (see `travel-72h.md`).

---

## Plants, spices, fats

- **Plants:** seasonal Indian veg (lauki, tinda, beans, cabbage, greens, gourds, tomatoes, cucumber) + fruit in season.
- **Legumes:** moong, masoor, chana, rajma, lobia — rotate; soak/pressure-cook for soft texture.
- **Fats:** mustard oil / groundnut / olive / ghee in measured spoons; nuts (almonds, walnuts, peanuts if no allergy) as garnish or small support.
- **Fish (when Principal File allows):** prefer well-cooked, known source; separate raw board (see pantry + WHO Five Keys).

## Hydration / caffeine / alcohol (lifestyle timing)

| Item | Chef / PA rule | Source takeaway |
|------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Water** | Visible bottle + room-temp or warm water on desk; chaas with lunch optional | NHS: fluids across day; water/tea/coffee count. [NHS water & drinks](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/food-guidelines-and-food-labels/water-drinks-nutrition/) |
| **Caffeine** | Prefer morning–early afternoon; avoid late push that fights sleep — default **stop ~8–12 h before planned sleep** unless Principal File says otherwise | Caffeine can disrupt sleep even 6 h pre-bed; newer work suggests longer windows. [Drake et al. J Clin Sleep Med](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3805807/); [Sleep Foundation overview](https://www.sleepfoundation.org/nutrition/caffeine-and-sleep) |
| **Alcohol** | Not a fuel strategy; if served, earlier evening, stop **≥3–4 h before sleep** when possible; never “for jet lag” | Alcohol fragments sleep; avoid close to bed. [Sleep Health Foundation](https://www.sleephealthfoundation.org.au/sleep-topics/caffeine-food-alcohol-smoking-and-sleep); [NHS sleep tips](https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/how-to-fall-asleep-faster-and-sleep-better/) |

## Day-type map (Forge | Travel | Stress | Peak)

### Forge (home / HQ training day)

- **First meal:** protein + plants + spice; steady, not heavy.
- **Midday:** substantial plate — dal/fish/paneer + 2 veg + millet/roti; dessert optional small.
- **Evening:** lighter protein + veg; earlier finish if session next morning.
- **Supports:** nuts, fruit, chaas, herbal tea.
- **Chef watch:** consistent meal windows; no random tasting carts.

### Travel (flight / arrival / first 72 h)

- **First meal on local day:** protein-forward, familiar India-kitchen flavours; modest volume.
- **Midday:** warm, digestible (khichdi-style / soft dal + veg); skip heavy tasting menus on landing day.
- **Evening:** early local dinner; avoid late hotel banquet.
- **Caffeine/alcohol:** tighten — caffeine earlier local AM; alcohol minimal or skip first night.
- **Detail:** `travel-72h.md`.

### Stress (high-stakes calendar / board / negotiation)

- **First meal:** reliable protein + warm spice; avoid brand-new cuisine experiments.
- **Midday:** familiar plate the principal already likes; one decision, not a tasting flight.
- **Evening:** light, warm, low-drama; kitchen holds a refuse-safe backup.
- **Supports:** chaas, ginger-jeera tea, nuts — no stimulant stacking late.
- **Chef watch:** fewer choices, faster service, allergen certainty.

### Peak (high-output / session-adjacent day)

- **First meal:** protein-forward; easy to eat; not greasy.
- **Pre-session cue (Floor Runner):** light carb+protein 1–2 h before if Principal File allows — **diet cue only, not a PranaForge protocol**.
- **Post-session cue:** protein + plants + fluids within a comfortable window; warm preferred.
- **Evening:** restore familiar Forge pattern; early if sleep is the priority.
- **Never invent** session protocols beyond Principal File + these cues.

## Chef operating rules (all day types)

1. Read Principal File likes/dislikes/allergens/Jain/onion-garlic **before** mise.
2. Physician/RD note beats this file.
3. One protein anchor per main; plants visible; spice native.
4. Warm service preferred for Travel/Stress evenings unless principal asks cold.
5. Log refusals (see `maintenance-sop.md`); do not re-offer refused item same day.
6. Claims Warden language only in cards and guest copy (`claims-safe-language.md`).
