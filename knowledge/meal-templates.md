# Meal Templates — Chef-Executable Cards

## Breakfast (4)

### 1. Moong Cheela Stack with Hung Curd
- **Day types:** Forge · Stress · Peak
- **Dish:** Soft moong cheelas; side hung curd; cucumber–tomato; optional mint chutney (no onion if restricted).
- **Kitchen notes:** Soak moong overnight; batter thin; medium tawa; cook through; serve warm.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → besan cheela (if gluten-free needed and besan OK) → oats pancake (egg or flax binder per Principal File).
- **[Stress]** Single plate, no buffet choice architecture.

### 2. Paneer Bhurji + Millet Toast
- **Day types:** Forge · Peak · Travel (day 2+)
- **Dish:** Soft paneer bhurji with turmeric–jeera–tomato; 1–2 millet or whole-wheat toasts; fruit cup.
- **Kitchen notes:** Low oil; soft curd cheese crumble; finish coriander; toast dry or light ghee.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → egg bhurji (if egg OK) → tofu bhurji (if soy OK) → masala oats + nuts.
- **Milk allergen:** refuse entire dish; use egg/dal path.

### 3. Vegetable Dalia / Broken-Wheat Upma (protein finish)
- **Day types:** Forge · Travel · Stress
- **Dish:** Vegetable dalia; finish with roasted peanuts *or* a side of dahi *or* soft boiled egg (one only, per file).
- **Kitchen notes:** Soft texture; ginger–jeera tadka; no heavy frying.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → soft moong khichdi → oats porridge + nuts.
- **[Travel-arrival]** Prefer this or #4 over fried hotel breakfast.

### 4. Soft Egg + Spinach + Fruit (or Jain egg-free: Dal Chilla)
- **Day types:** Peak · Forge
- **Dish:** Soft scramble or boiled eggs; lightly wilted spinach/methi; seasonal fruit; optional small roti.
- **Kitchen notes:** Eggs cooked through; separate board; greens washed.
- **Refuse-substitutes (no egg):** → **Dal chilla + dahi** → paneer scramble.
- **[Stress]** Pre-plated; zero “how do you like your eggs?” debate if preference already logged.

## Lunch (4)

### 5. Home-Style Dal Tadka + Jeera Rice + Two Sabzis
- **Day types:** Forge · Stress · Peak
- **Dish:** Toor or moong dal tadka; measured jeera rice *or* millet; dry sabzi + gravy sabzi; salad/raita.
- **Kitchen notes:** Dal fully cooked soft; tadka with jeera/hing/haldi; oil measured; serve hot >60°C hold if delayed.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → sambar + millet → rajma (if liked) → chana masala.
- **[Stress]** Exact familiar recipe from Principal File “likes”; no fusion twist.

### 6. Grilled Fish + Turmeric Veg + Soft Roti  **[fish gate]**
- **Day types:** Forge · Peak
- **Dish:** Simple grilled/steamed fish; turmeric–ginger veg tray; 1 soft roti; lemon.
- **Kitchen notes:** Separate raw board; cook thoroughly; juices clear; serve immediately.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → **paneer tikka plate** → chicken grill (if allowed) → double-dal thali.
- **[Travel-arrival]** Only if principal explicitly wants fish *and* kitchen trust is high; else paneer/dal.

### 7. Paneer Tikka Bowl + Salad + Chaas
- **Day types:** Forge · Travel · Stress
- **Dish:** Paneer tikka (tandoor or oven); cucumber–onion* salad (*omit onion if restricted); mint yogurt; chaas.
- **Kitchen notes:** Marinate dahi+spice; high heat; don’t dry out; warm service.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → soya chaap *only if soy OK & liked* → grilled mushrooms + dal cup.
- **[Travel-arrival]** Excellent default when dairy OK.

### 8. Khichdi Supreme (Moong + Veg) + Side Pickle/Papad restraint
- **Day types:** Travel · Stress · Forge recovery
- **Dish:** Soft moong–rice khichdi with carrots/beans/lauki; ghee spoon optional; cucumber; tiny pickle if Principal File allows.
- **Kitchen notes:** One-pot; soft; warm; avoid banquet sides sprawl.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → vegetable dalia → clear moong soup + toast.
- **[Travel-arrival]** **[Stress]** Primary landing/high-stakes lunch template.

## Dinner (4)

### 9. Light Fish Curry + Cauliflower + Soft Rice  **[fish gate]**
- **Day types:** Forge · Peak (early dinner)
- **Dish:** Light coconut or tomato fish curry; dry cauliflower/beans; half portion rice.
- **Kitchen notes:** Cook fish through; gravy not heavy cream bombs unless liked; serve early evening.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → light paneer curry → chicken stew (if OK) → dal + sabzi only.
- **Alcohol pairing:** not required; if served, earlier (see `travel-72h.md` / diet principles).

### 10. Warm Lauki–Moong Bowl + Roti
- **Day types:** Travel · Stress · Forge evening
- **Dish:** Lauki + moong gently spiced; 1 roti; dahi optional.
- **Kitchen notes:** Soft, warm, low drama; hing/jeera if no onion-garlic.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → tinda–moong → plain dal + sautéed greens.
- **[Travel-arrival]** **[Stress]** Preferred arrival-night dinner.

### 11. Grilled Chicken or Paneer + Mixed Sabzi + Millet
- **Day types:** Peak · Forge
- **Dish:** Simple grilled protein; colourful sabzi; millet or quinoa-style local millet.
- **Kitchen notes:** No heavy cream finish; herbs/spices for flavour; plate half plants.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** chicken↔paneer↔dal cake as per gates.
- **[Stress]** Lock protein from Principal File before shopping.

### 12. Clear Soup + Soft Idli or Appam + Coconut Chutney (light)
- **Day types:** Travel · Stress
- **Dish:** Vegetable or mild chicken clear soup; 2–3 soft idlis *or* appam; coconut chutney (fresh).
- **Kitchen notes:** Soup piping hot; batter trusted/fresh; chutney refrigerated after service.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → khichdi (#8) → dalia (#3 as early dinner).
- **[Travel-arrival]** When chewing fatigue / late landing.

## Supports (4+)

### 13. Ginger–Jeera Warm Water / Tea
- **Use:** between meals; Stress desk; Travel afternoon.
- **Kitchen notes:** Fresh ginger bruise; jeera lightly roast optional; no sugar default.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → plain warm water → tulsi tea if liked.
- **Claims:** “settling / warm comfort” — never “detox/cure”.

### 14. Handful Nuts + Fruit
- **Use:** Forge / Peak mid-window; not late-night Travel.
- **Kitchen notes:** Allergen check; unsalted; portion small cup.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → roasted chana → dahi cup.

### 15. Chaas / Buttermilk (plain)
- **Use:** lunch accompaniment; hot climate Forge.
- **Kitchen notes:** Fresh dahi + water + roast jeera; salt light; serve cool.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** → coconut water plain → lime water (no sugar bomb).

### 16. Pre/Post Session Fuel Cue (Floor Runner — **cue only**)
- **Pre (if Principal File allows):** small paneer/egg/nuts + fruit or toast **1–2 h before** session window — light, not greasy.
- **Post:** protein + plants + fluids warm (templates #7, #10, or #5 half-portion) within a comfortable window.
- **Refuse:** tasting menus, alcohol, large caffeine hits in the pre window.
- **Never invent** timed “protocols,” powders, or medical recovery claims.

### 17. Jain / No-Onion-Garlic Thali Variant
- **Any day type when flagged**
- **Dish:** Moong dal (hing tadka) + lauki/tinda/cabbage + rice/roti + fruit + dahi if dairy OK.
- **Kitchen notes:** Separate utensils from onion-garlic line; verify no hidden paste.
- **Refuse-substitutes:** hotel “veg” banquet (unsafe cross-contact) → private kitchen only.

## Sources (pattern-level)

- [ICMR-NIN DGI 2024](https://nin.res.in/dietaryguidelines/pdfjs/locale/DGI_2024.pdf) — pulses, dairy, cereals/millets, vegetables as Indian balanced-diet building blocks.
- [NIN My Plate brief](https://nin.res.in/brief/Policy%20Brief%20My%20Plate%20J18%2024.pdf) — plant-heavy plate proportion cue.
- [WHO Healthy diet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet) — legumes, whole grains, veg/fruit, mixed proteins.
- [Harvard Anti-Inflammatory Diet review](https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/healthy-weight/diet-reviews/anti-inflammatory-diet/) — fish, spices, plants, limit fried/ultra-processed — used as **pattern** inspiration for templates, not disease treatment.
- [WHO Five Keys](https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food/) — cook thoroughly, safe temperatures, separate raw/cooked (applied in kitchen notes).
