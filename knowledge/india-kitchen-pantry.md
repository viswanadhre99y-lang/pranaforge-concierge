# India Kitchen Pantry — Home & Hotel Private Kitchens

Chef-facing inventory cues for India-first UHNW service.  
**Physician-prescribed diets always win.** Confirm Principal File before every shop.

---

## Usually OK (India home / hotel private kitchen)

### Proteins & dairy
| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Moong, masoor, toor, chana, rajma, lobia | Soak / pressure-cook; rotate |
| Paneer (fresh) | High-heat sear or soft cube; check milk allergen |
| Dahi / hung curd / chaas | Plain first; flavoured only if Principal File allows |
| Eggs | Soft/hard/omelette; separate raw handling |
| Fish (when allowed) | Firm white or local oily fish — **Principal File gate**; cook thoroughly |
| Chicken (when allowed) | Bone-in or fillet; known supplier |
| Tofu / soya chunks | Only if soy allowed |

### Staples & carbs
| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Rice (sona masoori / basmati), millets (ragi, jowar, bajra, foxtail) | Prefer half whole/millet where principal accepts |
| Atta, besan | Fresh mill when possible |
| Oats | Steel-cut or rolled for first meal |
| Idli/dosa batter | Hotel-safe if freshly fermented / trusted kitchen |

### Vegetables & fruit (seasonal preference)
- Gourds (lauki, tinda, tori), beans, cabbage, cauliflower, spinach/methi/palak, carrots, beet, tomato, cucumber, capsicum, okra, eggplant
- Fruit: banana, papaya, pomegranate, apple, citrus, berries when clean/source-known
- Wash produce especially if eaten raw ([WHO Five Keys](https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food/))

### Spices & aromatics (default India kit)
- Haldi (turmeric), adrak (ginger), jeera, dhania, mustard seed, hing (asafoetida — **Jain/onion-garlic alternate**), black pepper, curry leaf, green chilli (heat to taste), cinnamon, cardamom, clove
- Fresh coriander / mint for finish

### Fats & nuts
- Mustard oil, groundnut oil, olive oil, measured ghee
- Almonds, walnuts, cashews, peanuts, pumpkin/sunflower seeds — **allergen check every time**

### Liquids
- Safe drinking water, pasteurized milk, buttermilk, coconut water (plain), unsweetened tea/coffee

## Common allergens (declare & separate)

FSSAI labelling expects declaration of: cereals containing gluten (incl. wheat), crustaceans, milk & milk products, eggs, fish, peanuts, tree nuts, soybeans, and sulphites ≥10 mg/kg. ([FSSAI Labelling & Display](https://www.fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/Comp_Labelling%20Display_Version%20VIII_09_09_2025.pdf); [FSSAI FAQ](https://fssai.gov.in/faqs/?p=1778))

**UHNW kitchen practice (US/EU guests may also expect sesame):**
1. Read Principal File allergen list every service.
2. Separate boards/knives for fish, egg, nuts, gluten where needed.
3. When in doubt: **refuse the dish**, offer a clean substitute, log it.

Major allergen framework mindset for establishments: train staff, prevent cross-contact, communicate ([FDA Food Code allergen framework refs via AFDO/HACCP materials](https://www.afdo.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/The-HACCP-Guide-11.28.23.pdf)).

## Onion / garlic / Jain notes

| Preference | Kitchen action |
|------------|----------------|
| **No onion / no garlic** | Use hing, cumin, ginger (if allowed), tomato, curry leaf; label clearly |
| **Jain (strict)** | No root veg below ground (onion, garlic, potato, carrot, beet, ginger* — *confirm*; no eggs/meat/fish; no honey sometimes — **ask Principal File** |
| **Satvik / temple days** | Confirm calendar; often no onion/garlic + no meat/egg |
| **Hotel banquet** | Do not assume banquet line is Jain-safe; cook separate in private kitchen |

Always prefer **one confirmed preference string** from Principal File over guessing.

## Paneer vs fish swaps (protein anchors)

| If… | Then serve… | Refuse / avoid |
|-----|-------------|----------------|
| Fish allowed & liked | Simple grilled/steamed/masala fish + veg | Raw fish unless Principal File + trusted sushi program |
| Fish refused / Jain / allergen | Paneer bhurji, paneer tikka, soft paneer curry, grilled paneer | “Fishy” oils that cross-contact |
| Dairy refused | Egg (if allowed) / dal + nuts / tofu (if soy OK) / chicken (if OK) | Paneer “hidden” in gravies |
| Egg refused | Dal + paneer or fish per above | Egg wash on “veg” snacks |

**Rule:** one clear protein anchor; never silent swap without PA/principal awareness on Peak/Stress days.

## Warm vs cold service

| Context | Prefer | Why (lifestyle, not medical) |
|---------|--------|------------------------------|
| Travel arrival night | **Warm** soft plate | Familiar, easy, hotel-safe |
| Stress evening | **Warm** | Low decision load |
| Forge lunch (India heat) | Warm mains + cool sides (raita, salad) OK | Comfort + freshness |
| Peak post-session | Warm protein + plants | Steady felt-energy cue |
| Cold platters | Only if Principal File likes; keep ≤2 h at room temp then refrigerate | WHO: don’t leave cooked food >2 h at room temp; keep hot >60°C or cold <5°C. [Five Keys PDF](https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/food-safety/five-keys-to-safer-food-poster/5keys-en.pdf) |

## Food safety — private / hotel kitchen (non-negotiable)

WHO Five Keys: (1) Keep clean (2) Separate raw & cooked (3) Cook thoroughly (4) Safe temperatures (5) Safe water & raw materials. ([WHO Five Keys](https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food/))

**Chef checklist**
- [ ] Hands/surfaces washed; pest-controlled area
- [ ] Raw fish/meat/eggs never share ready-to-eat boards
- [ ] Cook poultry/fish thoroughly; reheat leftovers piping hot
- [ ] Hot hold >60°C; cold <5°C; discard room-temp cooked food after ~2 h
- [ ] Wash fruit/veg; use safe water; prefer pasteurized dairy
- [ ] Allergen mise labelled

Traveller food-safety mindset: choose cooked, hot, known kitchens over street unknowns when principal is Travel-day sensitive ([WHO traveller food-safety adaptation of Five Keys](https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food/)).

## Hotel banquet vs private kitchen

| Banquet line | Private kitchen |
|--------------|-----------------|
| High cross-contact risk (buffet) | Controlled allergens |
| Hard to verify Jain/onion-garlic | Exact mise |
| Tasting-menu sprawl on landing | Single composed plate |

**Default for Travel/Stress:** private kitchen plate. Banquet only if Principal File allows and Claims Warden copy stays lifestyle-safe.
